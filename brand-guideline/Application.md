# Clight Brand System — Application Guidelines & Governance

## 1. Clear Space (Safe Exclusion Zone)
- Minimum margin surrounding the logo: **$1X$** ($42\text{ px}$ on standard grid).
- No interface text, graphic elements, or image boundaries may cross the exclusion line.

## 2. Minimum Reproduction Sizes
- **Digital Display**: $16 \times 16\text{ px}$ (Browser Favicon & System Tray).
- **Mobile Touch Icon**: $180 \times 180\text{ px}$ (iOS) / $192 \times 192\text{ px}$ (Android).
- **Physical Print**: $5.0\text{ mm}$ height with $1200\text{ DPI}$ vector output.

## 3. Application Showcase Contexts
1. **Browser Favicon**: Ultra-crisp 16/32px micro-crescent on light and dark browser tabs.
2. **Website Header**: Minimalist navigation bar with logo paired with wordmark "Clight".
3. **App Icon**: Light squircle and Dark squircle icons with precision optical centering.
4. **Documentation Sidebar**: Clean developer portal with search and hierarchical tree navigation.
5. **Mobile Splash Screen**: Serene portrait loading screen with high breathing room.
6. **Social Avatar**: Precision circular crop with $20\%$ radial padding.
7. **Signage Card Mockup**: Heavy textured cotton paper with tactile debossed foil stamp.
8. **Merchandise**: Ceramic coffee mugs, tote bags, and hardware badges.

## 4. Brand Governance (Do's & Don'ts)
- **DO**: Maintain standard $1:1$ aspect ratio.
- **DO**: Use official brand colors (#111111 on light, #FFFFFF on dark).
- **DON'T**: Stretch, squeeze, or skew the curve.
- **DON'T**: Rotate the logo or change opening direction arbitrarily.
- **DON'T**: Apply drop shadows, glows, gradients, or 3D extrusions.
- **DON'T**: Add decorative stroked outlines or frames around the curve.
